Service Quote

Your price has two parts: installation labor and the hair ordered for you. Hair is charged at $15 per gram for the full amount ordered, not only the amount installed. Labor is billed by the hour at your artist’s rate. A typical install uses about 100 grams of hair and about 3 hours of labor, for a typical total of about $3,000 with Nicole or $2,625 with Olivia. Your final quote is set at consultation.

Deposit

The deposit is $1,500 (100 grams × $15 per gram). It is due at booking and any time new hair needs to be ordered. Hair is custom ordered and color matched for you, so it cannot be returned or resold.

  • Your appointment is not confirmed and hair will not be ordered until the deposit is paid.
  • The deposit is credited to your total on the day of service.
  • The deposit is non-refundable once hair has been ordered.
  • If your order is more than 100 grams, the extra hair ($15 per gram over 100 g) is due at your install appointment. If your order is less than 100 grams, the unused part of the deposit is credited toward your labor.
  • Any hair from your order that is not installed is yours to take home.
  • A credit card must be kept on file at The Vault to book and hold your appointment.

Cancellation, Rescheduling & No-Shows

SituationWhat happens
Reschedule with 7+ days’ noticeDeposit transfers to the new date. One reschedule is allowed; any further change is treated as a late cancellation.
Reschedule or cancel with less than 7 days’ notice$500 fee charged to your card on file, unless The Vault is able to fill your appointment time. Deposit is non-refundable and does not transfer. Your hair is yours to pick up or bring to a new appointment.
No-show$500 fee charged to your card on file. Deposit is non-refundable and does not transfer. Your hair is yours to pick up or bring to a new appointment.
Arriving 15+ minutes lateYour service may be shortened or rescheduled. Arriving 30+ minutes late is treated as a no-show, including the $500 fee.
Salon must rescheduleDeposit transfers in full to a new date.

Card on file. A credit card must be on file at The Vault for every booking. By signing this agreement, you authorize The Vault to charge a $500 fee to that card for a cancellation with less than 7 days’ notice (the week of your appointment) or a no-show. For a late cancellation, the fee is waived if The Vault is able to fill your appointment time. The fee is separate from your deposit. Color appointments are all-day bookings, so this policy applies to them in full.

Hair pickup. Hair ordered for you belongs to you. If you cancel and do not rebook, you have 30 days from the cancellation date to pick it up. Hair not picked up within 30 days becomes part of The Vault’s overstock, and the deposit is not refunded.
