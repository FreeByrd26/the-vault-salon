Appointment Cancellation & No-Show Policy

We kindly ask for advance notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. Because each appointment is reserved exclusively for you, late cancellations and no-shows affect our stylists and other clients waiting for that time slot. Notice requirements and fees are based on the length of your scheduled service.

How Cancellation Fees Work

  • Card on file: A credit card is required to reserve all appointments. Your card will only be charged in the event of a late cancellation or no-show, as described in this policy.
  • Notice: We ask for at least 7 days’ notice to cancel or reschedule. A cancellation or reschedule with less than 7 days’ notice (the week of your appointment), or a no-show, carries a $500 fee charged to your card on file. For a late cancellation, the fee is waived if we are able to fill your appointment time.
  • How to cancel: Cancellations or changes must be made by phone, text, or through GlossGenius. Notice is effective at the time it is received, not when the appointment was originally scheduled.
  • Late arrivals: If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, your service may be shortened or rescheduled at the stylist’s discretion. Arriving 30 or more minutes late is treated as a no-show, including the $500 fee.

K-Tip Extension Deposits

K-Tip extension appointments require a $1,500 hair deposit at booking (100 grams × $15 per gram). Hair is custom ordered and color matched for you, so the deposit is non-refundable once hair has been ordered. The deposit is credited to your total on the day of service. With 7 or more days’ notice, one reschedule is allowed and your deposit transfers to the new date. Full terms, pricing, and the required agreement are on our K-Tip Deposit & Booking Agreement — see Client Forms.

Deposit terms for other extended or specialty services (bridal, color correction, Invisible Bead Extensions®) are confirmed at booking.

Privacy

This website uses no cookies, no analytics, and no tracking. Display fonts are served by Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address in the course of serving them.

Our client forms (such as the K-Tip agreement) collect the information you enter — name, contact details, and appointment details — solely so we can prepare your service. Submitted forms are delivered to The Vault by email through FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service, which processes your submission under its own privacy policy. We do not sell or share this information, and we never ask you to enter payment card numbers into a form on this site.

Appointments are booked through GlossGenius, our online booking provider. Any information you provide while booking (name, contact details, card on file) is collected and processed by GlossGenius under its own privacy policy and terms.

Questions? Contact us at nicole@thevaultnewcanaan.com or (203) 228-5027.

Exceptions

We understand that emergencies and illness happen. If you cancel outside of the required notice window due to illness, a doctor’s note will be required for the late cancellation fee to be waived. Other exceptions are considered on a case-by-case basis at the discretion of The Vault By Nicole Strachan.

By booking an appointment with The Vault By Nicole Strachan, you acknowledge and agree to this cancellation policy.
